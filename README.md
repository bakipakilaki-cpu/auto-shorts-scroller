## 🚀 Key Features

1. **⚡ Auto-Scroll on Video Finish**: Seamlessly glides to the next video when the current Short ends.
2. **🛡️ Auto-Skip Sponsored Ads**:
   - Immediately detects sponsored Shorts reels (`ytd-ad-slot-renderer`, `ad-badge-view-model`, CTA buttons).
   - Mutes audio instantly and auto-scrolls past the ad with zero delay.
   - Fast-forwards in-player video ads at 16x speed and auto-clicks "Skip Ad" buttons.
3. **⌨️ Instant <kbd>G</kbd> Key Skip**: Press <kbd>G</kbd> anywhere to skip to the next video instantly (safely ignores when typing comments or search).
4. **❤️ Auto-Like System**:
   - Automatically likes favorite creators (e.g. `@MrBeast`) or all watched Shorts after 2.5s.
5. **📜 Watch History**:
   - Automatically records viewed Shorts in the popup tab for easy re-watching.
6. **💎 VIP Activation Link Gate (Monetization)**:
   - Built-in license verification allows you to sell access and give buyers an activation link or key to open and unlock the extension.

## 🛠️ Installation Instructions

### Chrome / Brave / Edge:
1. Download [`auto-shorts-scroller.zip`](https://github.com/bakipakilaki-cpu/auto-shorts-scroller/releases/latest/download/auto-shorts-scroller.zip) and unzip it.
2. Navigate to `chrome://extensions` in your browser.
3. Enable **Developer mode** in the top-right corner.
4. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left) and select the `publish` folder.
5. Open [YouTube Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/) and refresh the page (<kbd>Cmd + R</kbd> on Mac or <kbd>Ctrl + R</kbd> on Windows).
