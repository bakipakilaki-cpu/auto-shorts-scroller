# Auto Shorts Scroller for YouTube ⚡ (v1.5.0 Pro Suite)

A complete YouTube Shorts productivity suite that auto-scrolls completed Shorts, automatically skips sponsored ads, lets you skip instantly with <kbd>G</kbd>, automatically likes your favorite creators, and tracks your watched Shorts history.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 1. 🛡️ Auto-Skip Ads (New in v1.5.0)
- **Instant Sponsored Ad Skipping**: As soon as a sponsored Short ad appears in the feed, it is skipped instantly with zero delay.
- **In-Player Ad Skip Button Clicks**: Automatically clicks standard "Skip Ad" buttons inside video players.
- **Toggle in Settings**: Turn Auto-Skip Ads on or off in the popup controls anytime.

### 2. ⚡ Auto-Scroll & Instant Skip
- **Auto-Scroll on Finish**: Advances to the next Short when the current video finishes.
- **Skip with `G`**: Press <kbd>G</kbd> anytime to skip directly to the next video (typing in comments and search is automatically safe).
- **Interactive Badge**: A sleek floating pill in the bottom-right corner (`🟢 Auto-Scroll ON`) lets you toggle auto-scrolling with a single click.

### 3. ❤️ Auto-Like Favorite Creators
- **3 Modes**:
  - **Off**: No automatic likes.
  - **Favorites Only**: Automatically likes Shorts from creators in your favorites list after 2.5 seconds of playback.
  - **All**: Automatically likes every Short you watch.
- **Creator Tag Manager**: Easily add or remove channel handles (e.g., `@MrBeast`) in the popup.
- **Safety First**: Never un-likes an already liked video, and never likes sponsored ads.

### 4. 📜 Recent Watched History
- Automatically logs Shorts you watch with title, creator handle, and timestamp.
- Browse your latest 30 watched Shorts right inside the popup.
- Click any card in the list to re-open that Short in a new tab.
- Includes a 1-click **Clear History** button.

## 📦 Download & Installation

### Option A: Use the Unpacked Folder (Fastest for Dev)
1. Open Google Chrome and go to `chrome://extensions`.
2. Enable **Developer mode** (top-right toggle).
3. Click **Load unpacked** (top-left button).
4. Select the [`publish`](file:///Users/brankopopovic/Documents/auto%20shorts%20scroller/publish) directory.
5. Open [YouTube Shorts](https://www.youtube.com/shorts/) and refresh the tab (<kbd>Cmd + R</kbd> on Mac).

### Option B: Use the Downloadable Packed ZIP
- Grab the packed archive: [`auto-shorts-scroller.zip`](file:///Users/brankopopovic/Documents/auto%20shorts%20scroller/auto-shorts-scroller.zip) in the root of this project.
- Unzip it anywhere and load it as an unpacked extension in Chrome!
